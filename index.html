Evidence scoring

Score each saved paper on three independent dimensions. This is a triage rubric, not a validated scientific quality score.

Evidence type: Review = 1; computational/observational = 2; controlled laboratory = 3; human clinical = 4.

Relevance: Indirect = 1; related mechanism = 2; directly tests the question = 3.

Replication: Unknown = 0; independently supported = 1.

Total: 2–8. Higher means prioritize reading, not proof of human lifespan extension.

This app does not train its own AI model, verify study findings, or recommend human biological interventions. It uses literature search and simple rule-based connection prompts. Future versions can add a cloud language model with human-reviewed citations.

Privacy and limitations

Saved papers and notes stay in this browser's local storage unless you export them. Clearing Safari site data may erase them. Search terms are sent to Europe PMC. Avoid entering private medical data.

Research tool only · No clinical advice · Verify papers and methods before drawing conclusions.
